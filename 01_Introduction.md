# 01 — Introduction: AI-Augmented Business Analysis

---

## The Shift in Plain English

2024: BAs used AI to draft emails faster.  
2026: BAs are expected to write requirements *for* AI systems, govern AI outputs, and lead AI transformation from the business side.

The role hasn't gone away. It's got harder — and more valuable.

---

## What Actually Changes (and What Doesn't)

| BA Task | Without AI | With AI + CRAFT | What You Still Own |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Elicitation prep | 2 hrs building question set | 10 min prompt → review | Stakeholder relationships |
| BRD first draft | Full day writing | 30 min prompt → validate | Accuracy, sign-off |
| User stories | Sprint 0 takes a week | Stories + ACs in an hour | Priority, domain check |
| Gap analysis | Manual As-Is/To-Be tables | Structured table in minutes | Business judgment |
| AI system requirements | — | **This is yours entirely** | AI can't write its own reqs |

That last row is the point. **AI cannot define requirements for itself.** What an AI agent should do, when it should escalate, what counts as biased output — that is the BA's new frontier.

---

## Four Domains in This Playbook

- 🖥️ **Enterprise IT** — ERP upgrades, migrations, digital transformation
- 🛒 **Retail** — demand forecasting, CRM, returns, loyalty, e-commerce  
- 📡 **Telecom** — CRM migration, AI chatbots, network upgrades  
- 🚚 **Logistics** — route optimisation, WMS, last-mile delivery

Pick the domain closest to your role. Adapt the prompt. Use it today.

---

## Three Rules Before You Start

**Rule 1: CRAFT first, type second.**  
Fill in all five elements before submitting. Completing CRAFT often clarifies what you actually need.

**Rule 2: AI drafts. You decide.**  
Every output here is a starting point. Your domain knowledge is the quality gate.

**Rule 3: Save what works.**  
A prompt that produced great output is a reusable asset. Build your library.
